# Changelog

## 0.0.1

Initial release for Hytale **0.8.0-pre.6**.

- Boats leave foam wakes and produce sounds that respond to movement.
- Boats pitch gently as you row. The speed of the animation varies with the boat's movement.
- Stationary boats gently rock from side to side while the entire hull is over water.
- Press the interaction key (**F** by default) to pick up boats that are fully ashore or stranded at the shoreline. Boats that can still sail remain boardable. If the player's inventory is full, the boat drops as an item.
- Players can check and adjust their rowing sound volume with `/rowing_volume`
- Players can check and adjust the boats foam opacity with `/rowing_opacity`.
- Language support: English and Spanish.
